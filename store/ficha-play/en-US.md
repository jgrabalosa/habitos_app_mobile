## Nombre

Norday Habits: Daily Habits

## Descripción breve

Pressure-free habit tracker with Nori, a pet that grows with you

## Descripción completa

Norday Habits is a pressure-free habit tracker: your rhythm goes up when you follow through and comes down slowly when you slip, so one bad day doesn't erase everything you've built. And you're not doing it alone: Nori, your pet, grows with you.

📈 Habits without pressure
Many habit apps count streaks: miss a day and you're back to zero. Norday doesn't. Every habit has its own rhythm, which goes up when you do it and comes down slowly when you miss. What counts is your consistency over the weeks, not a perfect day. And if you need a break, get pauses in the shop and pick the habit back up whenever you like.

🌱 Habits and routines your way
Create as many habits as you want and organize them by category. Choose how they fit into your daily routine: every day, a few times a week, or on the specific days you decide. Not sure where to start? We suggest seven ideas. Check them off with a tap and watch your day and your week take shape.

🐣 Nori, your habit buddy
Every habit you complete helps Nori grow, from egg to hatchling to adult. Feed him with your points so he gains experience.

✨ An app your way
Three visual identities change the colors, typography, shapes and background of the whole app: Profundidad, a journey among constellations; Neotokyo+, neon on black; and Dulce, drawn in pencil. You pick one when you start; the others are yours to get with points in the shop. When you complete your day, a constellation lights up in Profundidad and a bouquet of flowers is completed in Dulce.

🏆 Points, achievements and rewards
Every habit you complete earns points that you can trade in the shop for identities, food for Nori and pauses. Unlock achievements for your days done and for your rhythm.

🔔 Reminders at your time
Choose when you want to be reminded of each habit and organize your day without relying on memory.

🌍 In Spanish, English and Portuguese.

For healthy habits, study, work or rest: Norday Habits isn't just another to-do list, it's a daily companion for building habits that last. Consistency, not perfection.
